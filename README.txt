FIKMAN EDITS — RESPONSIVE WEBSITE WITH LIGHT MODE
=================================================

This update starts from the Fikman Edits website version that credits Nabwana Cyrus (Sailas) as Founder and Creative Director.

THEMES
- Dark mode remains the original Fikman Edits dark navy / blue appearance and is the default.
- Light mode adds a complementary pearl-white, icy-blue and navy palette.
- Use the LIGHT / DARK control in the top navigation to switch themes.
- The visitor's selected theme is remembered in the browser when storage is available.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN
- Layout adapts for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
- Smaller phones use simpler single-column service and project layouts to avoid crowding.
- Portrait videos remain portrait and are click-to-play.

DEPLOY
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the extracted folder contents to your existing Netlify site, keeping index.html, styles.css, script.js and assets together at the site root.
3. Deploy and refresh your Netlify website.

Do not upload index.html alone. Keep the assets folder beside it.


COMMUNITY SIGNUP / MANUAL APPROVAL
The homepage includes a Netlify Forms signup named "fikman-community-signup". Each submission is stored in the site's Netlify Forms dashboard. To receive an email for every signup, open Netlify > your site > Forms > Form notifications (or Site configuration > Notifications > Form notifications) and add an email notification for nabwanacyrus@gmail.com, selecting the fikman-community-signup form. Test with a submission after deploying.

MANUAL APPROVAL
Review each submission in Netlify Forms. Only add people you approve to any mailing list you use. The form does not automatically subscribe people or send marketing emails. The approval and sending of future updates remain under your control.

SOCIAL LINKS
The five clickable social icons link to the supplied Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn profiles.
